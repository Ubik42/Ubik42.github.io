# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary visitors are technical-art leads, tools and pipeline engineers, AI-tool teams, recruiters, and collaborators evaluating whether Lucas Shen can turn real DCC and game-production problems into reliable AI-assisted tools.

They usually arrive with limited time. They need to understand the role, see concrete work, inspect technical depth, and find the relevant repository, case study, demo, or contact channel without reading a full résumé first.

## Product Purpose

This site is the personal front door for an AI Tool Technical Artist. It introduces Lucas as a person first, then connects that story to selected production tools, public engineering work, visual experiments, and the channels where he shares them.

Success means a visitor can answer these questions quickly:

- What kind of technical artist is Lucas now?
- Which production problems can he solve across DCC, engines, web, and AI workflows?
- Where does AI help, and where do deterministic rules remain authoritative?
- What screenshots, data flows, tests, repositories, or runnable artifacts prove the work?
- Where should the visitor go next for a deeper review?

The site is Chinese-first for domestic recruiting and portfolio review. English remains a complete, manually selectable content state for international visitors.

## Brand Personality

Precise, inventive, grounded.

The experience should combine the directness of a senior toolmaker's workbench with the clarity and pacing of a distinctive portfolio. It should feel technically capable and actively maintained, while letting real interfaces, diagrams, and evidence carry the impression.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI portfolio pages built from oversized slogans, gradient text, floating glass cards, particle fields, and vague claims.
- A résumé rendered as a long chronological webpage with no usable project evidence.
- A copied product dashboard that assumes visitors already understand the system.
- Dense internal-tool screenshots shown without explanation, hierarchy, or a clear review path.
- Decorative terminal aesthetics used as shorthand for engineering ability.
- Public pages that expose internal code, company paths, confidential assets, credentials, personal phone numbers, or private messaging accounts.

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with the person and current role.** The visitor meets Lucas, understands his motivation, and then sees the AI Tool TA practice.
2. **Connect interests to evidence.** Every major capability points to a real interface, artifact, diagram, repository, validation result, or public channel.
3. **Keep the toolmaker’s precision.** Clear structure and honest technical language support the personal narrative without turning the homepage into a dashboard.
4. **Explain the AI boundary.** Show where AI drafts, diagnoses, clusters, or explains, and where deterministic rules validate and execute.
5. **Build a durable index.** The homepage remains concise while case studies and the AI Tool TA Portfolio can grow independently behind it.
6. **Publish safely.** Company experience is expressed through transferable methods and synthetic public cases, not internal implementation details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Use semantic structure, visible focus states, keyboard-operable interactions, useful alternative text, and reduced-motion behavior. Responsive layouts must recompose for narrow screens rather than shrink desktop UI.
