# Personal Site Redesign Brief

## Goal

Rewrite the GitHub Pages homepage as a personal self-introduction for 沈裕焱 / Lucas Shen. The first impression should be a curious, candid, technically grounded person who builds AI-assisted tools for artists, not a product dashboard or an online résumé.

## Content order

1. Identity cluster: avatar and name remain one compact flex group at every viewport.
2. First-person introduction: current AI Tool TA role, DCC / engine / creative-workflow focus, and the motivation behind the work.
3. A little about me: personal path, interests, learning style, and the connection between art, engineering, games, and tools.
4. Things I make: selected DCC tools, AI workflows, and graphics / game experiments, using real public assets and honest descriptions.
5. Find me online: each channel explains what the visitor will find there.

## Recommended direction

- Voice: candid, engineered, curious.
- Palette: committed tactile cobalt blue, electric green identity accent, cool white, near-black, muted steel blue.
- Typography: condensed display face for identity plus a readable humanist sans for prose; Chinese and English receive equal layout treatment.
- Layout: personal narrative first, varied horizontal sections, few containers, strong rules and spacing instead of a repeated card grid.
- Motion: one orchestrated hero entrance, subtle connected-node movement, image reveals tied to project content, reduced-motion fallback.
- 3D: optional and subordinate. A small connected-tool scene may live inside the blue hero field, but it must never displace the introduction or avatar.
